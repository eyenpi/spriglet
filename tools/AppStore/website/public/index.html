What Mallow does

a little

QUIETCOMPANY.

Mostly, Mallow keeps you company. When you want to play, it’s soft, light, and lands on its feet.

It peeks.

Rests beneath your notch with its face showing, blinking and breathing beside your work.

It plays.

Invite it out, pick it up, let it go. It lands softly, or catches the notch on its way home.

It waits.

Long, quiet pauses. Empty space around it clicks straight through to your work.

A field guide to spriglets · Volume I

Mallow is the first.
It won’t be the last.

Spriglets are small, soft creatures that live at the top of your screen. Each has its own shape and habits. We’re starting with Mallow; more spriglets are planned.

Mallow

Mollis inclinavus (Spriglet, 2026)

Soft, lavender, and a little shy. Rests beneath the notch with only its face showing, blinking and breathing beside your work. Comes out when invited.

Habitat
Beneath the notch; upper right on displays without one.
Range
Apple silicon Macs, macOS 26+.
Diet
Nothing. No account, no network.
Temperament
Quiet pauses and small gestures.

Quiet by design

Quiet company
keeps quiet.

Spriglets live entirely on your Mac. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing sent anywhere by the app. Mallow stays at its small resting peek until you invite it out.

Local

No account, app server, analytics, or screen capture.

Gentle

Respects Reduce Motion, Low Power Mode, and display sleep.

Out of the way

Clicks pass through empty space. Hide or pause in Settings.
