# Acorn layered rest art proof

This is an **unpromoted art prototype**, generated from the editable Acorn v03
Blender scene. Shipping resources and playback remain unchanged. The original
scene, geometry, material, lighting, 448-pixel camera, 40 Cycles samples, and
denoised-RGB/raw-alpha settings remain the source of truth.

```sh
python3 tools/CharacterAssets/export_rest_rig.py
python3 tools/CharacterAssets/export_rest_rig.py --check
python3 -m unittest discover -s tools/CharacterAssets -p 'test_rest_rig_export.py'
# Optional Pillow dependency for the ignored, offline @2x inspection board:
python3 tools/CharacterAssets/export_rest_rig.py \
  --proof .build/rest-rig-v04/native-size-board.png
```

The exporter uses Blender's camera-ray visibility to isolate actual meshes while
keeping their participation in lighting. For the clean face plate only, eyes
are removed from all ray transport so their dark contact shadows do not remain
as an open-eye footprint underneath a closed blink or displaced pupil.
It does not cut regions out of the
beauty frame, erase eyes, invent flattened gaze grids, or add a correction image.
Each PNG has tight transparent bounds and two pixels of padding. All coordinates
are top-left pixels in the original 448 × 448 canvas. `framePixels` places the
crop; `pivotPixels` is a global source-canvas point, not a local crop coordinate.
The explicit top-left ground anchor converts the old manifest's bottom-left Y.

The hierarchy keeps feet and contact shadow on the ground. Body includes its
paws, brows, nose and mouth; cap contains the shell, lip and stem; the independent
leaf includes its midrib. The face retains Acorn's dark-glass style: each movable
eye image is the actual dark eye with its rendered highlight, not a newly added
white sclera. The source geometry's alpha supplies an experimental gaze mask.
That mask is inactive at neutral, avoiding alpha-squared antialiasing. Its small
gaze range and clipping appearance still require native artistic review.

The three fractional blink images per eye use the original `Blink` shape driver
and matte eyelid crease. They replace the open eye image and never overlay a
second complete eye. Metadata proposes a finite 160 ms blink and a single small
3.2 s breathing excursion. It does not authorize recurring animations. Static
boards cannot certify their timing or compositor cost.

## Honest boundary contract

`neutral.png` is generated by source-over compositing the real layer PNGs. The
validator recomposes it and requires identical decoded bytes. This proves a
new canonical layered pose is reproducible. It does **not** prove that it equals
the old monolithic v03 beauty image, nor that Core Animation's color management
will reproduce this encoded-sRGB reference byte for byte.

Independent Cycles passes and independent denoising change edge coverage and
shading slightly. Even rerendering the original whole scene may produce small
changes on another Blender/driver build. `boundaryVerification` therefore
records both measured differences and deliberately leaves
`productionHandoffApproved` false. There is no residual/correction image hiding
that discrepancy. Do not install this proof into production as a supposedly
pixel-perfect replacement for v03.

A production schema-3 package needs a coherent new canonical ready pose and
macro endpoints exported from that same declared composition, plus a native
handoff comparison showing zero pose/eye/foot jumps and no color-management
flash. The existing seven clips must remain usable until that migration passes.
`canonical-endpoints.json` proposes explicitly substituting this new canonical
at all ten ready junctions in a **new** package: both ends of idle and both walks,
the start of pet/fallAsleep, and the end of settle/wakeUp. The original schema-2
resources are untouched. The proposal retains frame counts, timing and scaled
root offsets, verifies each junction's original in-place skeletal/facial pose
and planted feet, and reports differences to both the old endpoint and its
adjacent authored frame. This is a deliberate new canonical definition, not a
claim that independently rendered layers equal the old beauty frame.
Sleeping still uses its authored baked pose. A layered sleep rig would require
a separately exported shared sleep canonical; it is not inferred from rest.

The ignored proof board shows all three 72/96/120-point canvases on light, dark,
and busy static backgrounds, at 2 output pixels per point. It is an offline
inspection aid, not a screenshot of native application presentation. Device
scale, light/dark edge fringing, motion legibility, process/WindowServer/GPU cost,
and long-term energy still require native acceptance. This artifact alone does
not claim artistic approval or continuous-animation energy acceptance.
